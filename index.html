Top bar before · bottom bar after. Working sets at RPE 8-9 only; warm-ups don't count.

Quads went down on purpose

9 sets to 7. Goblet squat and DB step-up were capped by grip and how much you can hold at your chest, not by your legs. Hack squat loads the quad directly and keeps progressing for years. Seven loadable sets beat nine grip-limited ones.

Chest at 7 is the remaining tight spot — enough to grow on, first place to add if this ever goes to 3 sessions.

Progression

Double progression. Top of the rep range on every set → add 5 lb upper / 10 lb lower next session. Otherwise stay at the same load and add reps.

Every 4th week: 2 sets per exercise at 60% load. Still show up.

Rotate variations every 8-12 weeks.

Fuel — E

~3000-3200 kcal/day, 120-140 g protein. Weigh in weekly, same conditions, morning. Flat for 3 weeks → add 200 kcal. Target +0.3-0.5 kg per month, no faster.

Creatine monohydrate 5 g/day. Sleep 7-8h — at 6h, protein synthesis drops measurably.

SkiErg Tabata

20s all-out / 10s rest × 8. Four minutes of work. SPM 45-55, power from the hip hinge and trunk through the lats.

Round 8 should be 25-40% below round 1. If it isn't, you paced it. Log average watts across all 8 rounds — that number is the progression metric, retest every 8-12 weeks.
